PROPOSITIONAL LOGIC MODEL

I = High income
C = Excellent credit
E = Permanent employment
L = No existing loans
R = Good repayment history
A = Loan approved
M = Manual review
J = Loan rejected

I AND C AND E AND L AND R -> A
MediumIncome AND GoodCredit AND E AND OneLoan AND R -> M
PoorCredit AND PoorRepayment -> J
TwoOrMoreLoans AND PoorRepayment -> J

For Giridhar, I, C, E, L and R are true; therefore A is true.
