FIRST-ORDER LOGIC MODEL

Predicates:
income(X, high)
credit(X, excellent)
employment(X, permanent)
loans(X, none)
repayment(X, good)
approved(X)
review(X)
rejected(X)

Rules:
FOR ALL X:
income(X, high) AND credit(X, excellent) AND employment(X, permanent) AND loans(X, none) AND repayment(X, good) -> approved(X).

FOR ALL X:
income(X, medium) AND credit(X, good) AND employment(X, permanent) AND loans(X, one) AND repayment(X, good) -> review(X).

FOR ALL X:
credit(X, poor) AND repayment(X, poor) -> rejected(X).

FOR ALL X:
loans(X, two_or_more) AND repayment(X, poor) -> rejected(X).
